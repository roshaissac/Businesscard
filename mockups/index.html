What ties them together (the regiment): the near-black cinematic hero · the warm-white text · the gold pill CTA · the "dawn seam" gradient into the ivory workspace · the sticky Dreamhouse brokerage band · one type system (system-sans + Fraunces for the single hero moment).

What makes each its own (the individuality): a signature accent rotated a few degrees around the brand orange, and a hero motif that says what the tool is — the living number (calculator), the threshold arch (intake), the porch-light marquee (open house), the five stars (reviews), the route tally (door-knock).

Note: these are static dummies for look approval only. Fraunces is loaded from a CDN here for preview — production self-hosts one woff2 to keep the zero-CDN rule. The cards (E-9/E-10) are shown as the reference and won't change.
